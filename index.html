About this tool

Patents Simple Family Tool

A lightweight tool for quickly finding patent family members across jurisdictions.

Broader family lookupFind family members across jurisdictions using EPO OPS, with direct links to Google Patents and Espacenet.

Supports examination and searchingQuickly identify earlier-published family members and English-language family members for citations used in examination and search reports.

Complements FMAProvides a focused interface for quick family lookup and use alongside FMA.

Independent backup pathwayRetrieves family listings through the European Patent Office Open Patent Services (OPS), providing an additional lookup option.
